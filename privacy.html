Privacy Policy — ClipesGTA6RPAIA

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Data we access

When the account owner authorizes ClipesGTA6RPAIA through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic), such as display name and avatar, used only to confirm which account is connected;
  • Permission to publish videos (video.publish) to that same account;
  • An access token and refresh token issued by TikTok.

The same applies to the Instagram and YouTube accounts the owner connects through Meta and Google.

2. How the data is used

Tokens are used only to upload videos to the owner's own accounts and to check the publishing status. Profile information is not used for any other purpose.

3. Storage

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. There is no external database and no data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties, other than the platform the video is being posted to.

4. Other people's data

The app does not collect data from viewers, followers or other TikTok users.

5. Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services) or in the Meta and Google account settings. Local tokens are deleted when access is revoked or on request to the contact below.
